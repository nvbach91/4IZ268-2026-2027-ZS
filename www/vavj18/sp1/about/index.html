About CS2TV

Who We Are

CS2TV is your ultimate destination for everything related to competitive Counter-Strike 2. Founded by passionate fans of the tactical shooter genre, our goal is to provide a centralized hub where the community can follow their favorite teams, track ongoing tournaments, and stay updated with the latest news in the esports scene.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make following top-tier Counter-Strike as seamless and engaging as possible. Whether you are a veteran player who remembers the early days of Global Offensive, or a newcomer experiencing the thrill of the Source 2 engine for the first time, CS2TV aims to deliver accurate, up-to-date, and comprehensive coverage of the global circuit.

What We Offer

  • Live & Upcoming Events: Detailed schedules, formats, and prize pools for Majors, IEMs, BLAST Premier, and tier-two tournaments.
  • Team Rosters & Statistics: Up-to-date active rosters and significant trophy cabinets for the best organizations in the world.
  • Breaking News: Roster shuffles, game updates, patch notes, and deep-dive analyses into the competitive meta.
